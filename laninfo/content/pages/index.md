## Getting started

1. **Connect.** Plug your PC into the switch (preferred) or join the Wi-Fi above. You get an address automatically (DHCP), no static IP needed.
2. **Get the games.** Every game on the [Games](/games) page can be downloaded right here at full LAN speed, no internet needed.
3. **Allow the firewall.** The first time a game hosts or joins, Windows asks about the firewall. Tick **Private networks** and allow, otherwise nobody finds your game. Your network should be set to *Private*, not *Public*.
4. **Join.** Dedicated servers run on `{{server_ip}}`. Most games list them in their LAN tab, otherwise join by IP.

> [!TIP]
> Use the **same player name** in every game, so everyone knows who just fragged them.
